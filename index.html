Nassos Daphnis

1914 – 2010 · New York
Artist

Born in Sparta, Greece, Daphnis immigrated to New York at 16. His WWII camouflage work sparked a lifelong obsession with flat color. Represented by Leo Castelli, his paintings entered MoMA, the Guggenheim, and the Whitney.

The Painting

From his SS Series — concentric vesica piscis ovals in industrial enamel, built with hard tape edges and zero brushstroke. Pure color, pure geometry.

Meaning
